Return and Cancellation Policy for Premium Collection By Sanjida

Thank you for shopping with Premium Collection By Sanjida. We value our customers and want to make your shopping experience as simple, transparent, and hassle-free as possible.

Please read this Return, Refund, Exchange, and Cancellation Policy carefully before placing an order.

1. Return & Refund Policy

We carefully check our products before dispatch. However, if you receive a product that is damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible.

Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged or defective product, please contact us with clear photos or videos of the product and packaging.

After reviewing the issue, we may offer:

  • A replacement product
  • An exchange
  • A refund, where applicable

The final resolution will depend on the condition of the product and the nature of the issue.

Incorrect Product

If you receive a product that is different from the product you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible.

After verification, we may arrange a replacement or exchange for the correct product.

You may be required to return the incorrect product before the replacement is sent.

2. Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return or exchange, the product should generally be:

  • Unused and unworn
  • In its original condition
  • Free from stains, damage, washing, or alterations
  • Returned with the original packaging, tags, or accessories where applicable

Products that have been used, washed, damaged by the customer, altered, or otherwise changed may not be eligible for return or exchange.

Certain products, including customized or personalized items, may not be eligible for return unless the product is received damaged, defective, or incorrect.

4. Refund Process

If your refund request is approved, the refund will be processed according to the applicable payment method and our verification process.

The time required to receive a refund may vary depending on the payment method or financial service provider.

Where applicable, refunds may be subject to deductions for delivery charges or other non-refundable costs.

5. Exchange Policy

If an eligible product needs to be exchanged, we will try to provide the requested replacement subject to product and size availability.

If the requested product or size is unavailable, we may offer an alternative solution where applicable.

Additional delivery charges may apply in certain exchange situations, depending on the reason for the exchange.
